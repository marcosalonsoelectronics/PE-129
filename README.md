This video is a continuation of previous video PE #128. Here we are applying the Full-FHA technique to obtain a circuit-based model of the LLC resonant converter. The implementation of this model in Qspice is also shown. The circuit-based FFHA model allows us to better visualize the relationship among the different variables of the model. In addition, we verified that the simulation of circuit-based model in Qspice is more efficient than that of the analytical models.
